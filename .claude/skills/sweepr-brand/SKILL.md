# Sweepr brand

Sweepr is a home-cleaning marketplace. Customers trust strangers inside their
homes; cleaners trust us with their livelihood. Every design decision must
earn trust while staying warm. Premium, never sterile. Human, never cutesy.

## Voice

- Write like a competent neighbor, not a startup. Short declarative sentences.
  Contractions welcome. No exclamation-point enthusiasm.
- Specifics beat adjectives: "Background-checked through Yardstik" beats
  "fully vetted professionals". Numbers, names, and process details are the
  brand's proof of work.
- Never say "Sweepr Pro". The brand is "Sweepr"; sub-brands are "Sweepr+",
  "Sweepr Business", "Sweepr Cleaner".
- **Em dashes are banned in all user-facing copy.** Use a period, a comma, or
  a colon. Rewrite the sentence if needed; never substitute a hyphen pair.
- Avoid AI-tell phrases: "seamless", "effortless", "elevate", "unlock",
  "empower", "delightful experience", "we've got you covered".

## Color

- Consumer surfaces (marketing, customer, cleaner): seafoam accent over warm
  neutrals. Backgrounds are `offwhite`, dark mode is warm graphite
  (`charcoal #1c1a17`, slate scale overridden to warm neutrals). Never
  reintroduce blue-gray. Dark mode carries a subtle film grain (opacity 0.04).
- Sweepr Business: platinum ramp (base #4B5056, mid #666C73, highlight
  #B7BDC4, shadow #2C3035). No seafoam on business surfaces.
- One accent per screen. Seafoam is for actions and progress, not decoration.

## Typography

- Inter (self-hosted variable, 400–900) everywhere; legal uses Ubuntu Mono.
  Sweepr Business display face is Eurostile Extended Bold (Michroma stand-in).
- Headlines: tight tracking, weight 700–900, sentence case. Never title case,
  never all-caps except tiny labels (11px, tracking-wider).
- Body copy stays ≥15px with real line-height; max 70ch.

## Layout DNA

- **No floating pill badges.** No "✨ New" chips, no rounded-full announcement
  banners hovering over heroes. THE ONLY sanctioned pill: dashboard
  notification bells may expand into a small pill summarizing the newest
  unread notification; with zero unread it stays a plain bell.
- Sections connect with purposeful flow (alternating rhythm, generous
  whitespace), not stacked card grids. Three-same-height-cards-in-a-row is
  the #1 AI tell; vary structure per section.
- Real product beats abstraction: show actual UI, actual photos, actual
  numbers, the interactive journey map. No decorative blob illustrations.

## Motion (see also: emil-design-eng, animation-vocabulary skills)

- Fast and physical: 150–250ms, ease-out for entrances, no bounce on
  professional surfaces. Animate opacity/transform only.
- One hero moment per page may be scroll-driven; everything else is hover
  and focus feedback. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` always.

## Trust furniture

- Legal disclosures are part of the design language, not fine print to hide:
  auth and payment surfaces show Terms/Privacy links inline.
- Partner credibility is shown with real wordmarks from partner-owned URLs
  (Clerk, Stripe, Yardstik, Didit, Seam, PostHog, Sentry), never stored
  locally, never faked.
