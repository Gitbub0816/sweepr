## Description
Sweepr is the easiest way to book a trusted home cleaning. Tell us about your
home, pick a date, and we match you with a background-checked professional
cleaner. Transparent pricing is calculated up front — no surprises.

What you can do with Sweepr:

- Book in minutes. Choose your package, cleaning level, and any add-ons, and get
  an exact price before you confirm.
- Track arrival live. On the day of your cleaning, follow your cleaner's approach
  on the map and know exactly when they'll arrive.
- Smart Entry. Grant secure, one-time access so your cleaner can get in even when
  you're not home — every unlock is authorized by Sweepr and fully logged.
- Manage everything. Reschedule, add a tip, leave a review, and see your full
  booking history in one place.
- Sweepr+ membership. Join for recurring savings and member perks on every
  cleaning.

Payments are handled securely by Stripe. Sweepr+ and all cleaning charges are for
real-world cleaning services performed at your home.

Cleaning available in select service areas — we're expanding fast.
