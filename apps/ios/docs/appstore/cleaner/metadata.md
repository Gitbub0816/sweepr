## Description
Clean with Sweepr is the app for professional cleaners who take jobs through
Sweepr. Everything you need for the day of service lives in one place.

With Clean with Sweepr you can:

- See your jobs. Review each assignment — address, scope, package, and any
  add-ons — before you head out.
- Get there. Open directions to the property in your Maps app and share your live
  arrival with the customer while you're on the way.
- Check in and out. Capture the required before-and-after photos and confirm your
  work directly in the app.
- Smart Entry. When a customer has enabled it, unlock their door at the property.
  Access is revealed only after you've checked in, gated by Face ID, and every
  unlock is authorized by Sweepr's servers.
- Track earnings. Watch your completed jobs and payouts add up.

Payouts are handled securely through Stripe. This app is for approved Sweepr
cleaning professionals.
